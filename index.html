Pet project / Product ownership / AI-assisted delivery / Agriculture

Agromega plant diary and planner

Since April 2026, I have owned the product direction for an early-stage agriculture web product. I designed the plant diary and planner from scratch: business processes, user flows, data logic, what information is collected, where it comes from, how it is displayed, and which features should be included first.

Dashboard

A daily overview with active plants, actions of the week, plants that need attention, seasonal harvest, and a practical daily recommendation.

Diary cards

Growing zones with plant groups, tags, last action, active plant count, action count, and fast actions for watering, harvest, photo, notes, and more.

History timeline

A chronological care log that groups actions by date, supports plant filters, shows disease and watering events, and connects actions with photos and AI advice.

Plant collection

Structured plant profiles with variety, custom name, planting date, plant age, diary links, status, last care action, and active/archive states.

This case shows the next stage of my workflow: using Codex as a product and development partner while keeping ownership of product decisions. I can take an unclear early-stage product and turn it into a working system: business logic, UX flows, data structure decisions, visual presentation, frontend tasks, AI-assisted development prompts, tests, QA checks, and iterative improvements.

Pet project / Product UX / AI-assisted prototype / Women's health

Luna cycle-life calendar

Luna is a mobile-first product prototype for a woman's daily rhythm around the menstrual cycle. I designed it as a calm personal space that turns cycle data into practical daily guidance without making the product feel medical, overwhelming, or judgmental.

Daily rhythm

A Today screen with cycle phase, day navigation, care plan, water goal, movement, fasting rhythm, mood tracker, and gentle reminders.

Nutrition logic

A vegan nutrition flow with meal logging, approximate macro analysis, daily totals, plant variety, and phase-aware food recommendations.

Fasting timer

A flexible night-break timer with 13, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, 36-hour, and custom plans, including clear safety messaging.

Cycle system

A 28-day calendar, four cycle phases, insights, profile settings, localStorage persistence, and mobile-first interaction patterns.

This project shows my product design and frontend thinking in a sensitive consumer-health context: reducing cognitive load, shaping daily scenarios, designing data presentation, writing careful microcopy, planning user flows, and using Codex-assisted development to move from idea to working prototype.
